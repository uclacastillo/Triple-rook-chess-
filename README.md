## Play

- **[Play in your browser](https://triple-rook-chess.vercel.app/)** on a phone, tablet, or computer.
- Choose **Play the computer** to play White against the computer's Black pieces.
- Choose **Two players** to share one device. Each player secretly chooses and locks a move, then both moves are revealed together.

## Noah's rules

1. **Start like chess.** Use a standard 8×8 board and the usual pieces.
2. **The king is the third rook.** Each side has two ordinary rooks and a king-rook. The king moves *only* like a rook: any number of empty squares vertically or horizontally.
3. **No check or checkmate.** The king-rook can be captured like any other piece. Losing it alone does **not** end the game.
4. **Move simultaneously.** Each player chooses a move from the same starting board position. Once both are locked, the moves resolve at the same time.
5. **Both pieces can die.** If both moving pieces choose the same destination, both are removed. If two opposing pieces simultaneously capture one another by swapping squares, **both are removed**.
6. **Capture all three original rooks to win.** That means the two starting rooks **plus** the king-rook. If both sides lose their last original rook in the same round, the game is a draw.

### Other prototype rules

- Other pieces keep their familiar movement patterns.
- Players may **pass** instead of moving. Three consecutive rounds where *both* pass produce a draw.
- Pawns can promote, but a promoted rook does **not** add another life to the original three.
- Castling and en passant are not included.
- White's pieces are shown in **blue**, Black's in **coral**, to make the sides easier to distinguish.
- The next round starts automatically after the reveal; there is no extra **Next Round** button.

These are the current prototype rules. **Noah gets the final say** on how his game evolves!

## Run it locally

This is a lightweight, self-contained website: no dependencies, build process, or accounts are needed.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
3. Pick a game mode and play!
